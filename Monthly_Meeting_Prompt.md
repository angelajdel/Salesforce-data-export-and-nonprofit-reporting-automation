# Monthly Meeting Prompt Template

Copy everything in the box below into a new chat with Claude each month,
paste your fresh numbers into the bracketed spots, and attach (or paste in)
your cleaned `Monthly_Report.xlsx` / cleaned CSVs. Claude will turn it into
talking points, a short narrative summary, and a slide-by-slide script you
can read from in the meeting.

---

```
You are helping a nonprofit prepare for its monthly board/donor meeting.

I'm attaching this month's cleaned Salesforce data (test records and
duplicates already removed): [attach Monthly_Report.xlsx or the cleaned
clients/donors CSVs].

Context:
- Reporting period: [e.g. February 2026]
- Programs we run: [e.g. Job Training, Housing Assistance, Food Security,
  Youth Mentorship]
- Anything unusual this month (a big grant, a staffing change, a new
  partnership, a program that struggled): [fill in or leave blank]
- Upcoming deadlines or asks I need the group to know about: [e.g. grant
  renewal due March 12, spring campaign kickoff March 20]
- Audience for this meeting: [board members / major donors / staff / funders]
- Tone: [concise and data-forward / warm and story-driven / a mix]

Please:
1. Summarize the month in 3-4 sentences a board member could read in
   15 seconds — clients served, dollars raised, and the one thing that
   most needs their attention.
2. Pull out 3-5 talking points I can speak to live, each with the
   supporting number from the data.
3. Flag anything in the data that looks off (a sharp drop, an
   unusually large single gift, a program with no new clients) so I can
   address it before someone asks.
4. Write a one-paragraph narrative I could read aloud to open the
   meeting, connecting the numbers to our mission.
5. List the deadlines and asks I gave you above as a clear action list,
   with owners if I specified any.

Keep it grounded in the actual numbers I've given you — don't estimate
or invent figures that aren't in the data.
```

---

### Tips for getting the most out of this each month

- **Keep the same shape every month.** Reusing this exact template makes
  it easy to compare month to month and spot trends Claude (or you) might
  otherwise miss.
- **Paste in last month's summary too** if you want Claude to highlight
  what changed since then.
- **Ask follow-ups in the same chat** — "make the opening paragraph
  shorter," "add a slide script for the donor chart," "turn talking point
  3 into a thank-you note for our top donor" — Claude will keep using the
  same data.
